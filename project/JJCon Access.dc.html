5 · Roles and permissions

Anything a role can't do is left out of the page entirely: no greyed-out Edit buttons, no locked menu items, no empty Settings group. Being assignable and being able to sign in are separate: everyone on the People list can own work, but only people with an accepted invitation can sign in.

Sessions "Keep me signed in" lasts 30 days per device and is on by default. Unticked, the session ends when the browser closes. The sidebar always shows who is signed in, with Sign out underneath. Admins can sign a person out of every device from their People record.
Deactivating Removes the login and hides the person from assignment dropdowns. Their record, task history and name on printed packets stay. Open work must be reassigned first. Reactivating restores their earlier role; they sign in with their old password.
